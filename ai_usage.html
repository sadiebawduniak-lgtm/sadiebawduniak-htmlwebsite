Correct AI Usage

The correct use of AI can be boiled down to one sentence: use AI as a tool to assist you, not as your brain. AI should be used as a way to fine tune thoughts and ideas, not to create ideas or do work for you. AI should be treated as any other website, as in it should be used as a resource to help you with work, decisions, and ideas but should not be depended on to run your life.

Your overall argument presents a grounded, practical framework for maintaining human agency and critical thinking while using technology. You make a strong point that relying entirely on AI for cognition or decision-making can diminish creativity, problem-solving skills, and personal responsibility. However, your assertion that AI "should not be used to create ideas or do work for you" represents a slightly narrow perspective on how collaboration with AI actually functions. An alternative viewpoint is that AI can serve as a powerful brainstorming partner—generating novel combinations of ideas, sparking creative pathways a human might not have considered, and automating tedious, repetitive tasks so individuals can focus on higher-level strategic thinking. Furthermore, comparing AI to "any other website" somewhat understates its interactive, generative capabilities, as traditional websites passively store information while AI dynamically processes and adapts to user input. Refining your argument to distinguish between passive dependency and active, iterative collaboration would make your overall thesis more nuanced and compelling.

Incorrect AI Usage

Making Day-to-Day Decisions
Students may use AI to decide what colleges to apply to, what to eat for breakfast, and many other small decisions like these. This can cause students to lose their intuition and autonomy, as well as teach students to rely heavily on AI for basic tasks of living.

Spreading Misinformation
Students may use AI for historical facts or scientific facts and put these facts into presentations or essays without cross-checking against verified sources. This could cause the student to unintentionally spread misinformation

Plagiarism
Students might insert an assignment or prompt into AI then directly copy and paste AI’s answer as their essay. AI can also be used to ghostwrite.

The incorrect use of AI can be incredibly dangerous. If a person is relying on AI for day-to-day decisions, that person is losing their ability to think for themselves. If a person is using AI for every assignment in college, that person will be unable to perform in a professional setting. I have also started seeing people online being entrapped in “AI Psychosis.”
